# PC diagnostics notes

My notes for tracking computer symptoms instead of guessing parts. Mostly BIOS/UEFI checks, storage, RAM, and how to write down what changed after a repair.

## My troubleshooting order
1. Write down the symptom and whether it is repeatable.
2. Check power, connections, and error messages first.
3. Review BIOS/UEFI detection and basic health information.
4. Change one variable at a time and record the outcome.
5. Confirm the machine boots and the original symptom is gone.

`INVENTORY_TEMPLATE.csv` and `SERVICE_TICKET_TEMPLATE.md` are blank formats; they don't describe completed repairs.

Don't open a power supply, UPS, or other high-voltage equipment. Disconnect power and follow proper ESD handling before working inside a PC.
